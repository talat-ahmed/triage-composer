Why the wording is like this

Each message is built to satisfy two sets of rules at once: the GP contract's access requirements and the GMC's guidance on delegating to colleagues.

The GP contract, 2026/27

From April 2026 practices must deal with requests they identify as clinically urgent on the same day, give non-urgent requests an appropriate response by the end of the next core-hours period, keep online consultation open throughout core hours without capping requests, and must not ask patients to call back, or make contact, on another day. An "appropriate response" is one of four things: an appointment (in person, telephone or video) at an appropriate and reasonable time; advice or care by another method; directing the patient to an appropriate service, including one they can access themselves; or a request for further information. Every outcome in the composer maps to one of the four, and when a patient cannot take the slot offered the fallback is always something the practice does or the patient can do now: self-book on the NHS App, or the team books the next suitable slot. "Try again another day" never appears.

GMC guidance on delegation

A message to the team is a delegation. The GMC's Delegation and referral guidance asks for clear instructions covering what needs to be done, by whom and by when; the delegating clinician remains accountable for the decision and for the patient's overall management, and must record the work in line with Good medical practice paragraphs 69 to 71. So every message names the action, the clinician type (and the person, where it matters) and the timeframe, and the same-day path tells the team to escalate straight back to you if there is no slot. The optional safety-net line gives the team a consistent form of words to relay, so the patient always knows what to do if things change before they are seen.

Deliberate wording choices

Fallbacks use self-book or next-slot routes, never "resubmit another day". A timeframe is stated in every booking, with the contract's three tiers (today, next soonest, when next available) plus a specific clinic day. Same-day bookings carry an escalation line. A safety-net line the team relays is available on clinical outcomes and on by default. NHS App education asks the team to confirm a slot is secured before the call ends. Optional "record on EMIS" and "no text message" lines are available everywhere they make sense. Nothing you type is sent anywhere: the page runs entirely in your browser and only your own name and settings are remembered, on your device.

Klinik's character limit

Set your Klinik message limit in Settings (500 by default). When a message runs over, the composer swaps in shorter phrasing sentence by sentence, least important first, and warns you if it still does not fit.
